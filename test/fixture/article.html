A short article about reading aloud

People have read long pages out loud for as long as there have been pages. A good read-along keeps the words on the screen in time with the voice, so your eyes can rest on the sentence you are hearing. This fixture is only here so the extension can be tested without opening a public website.

The first paragraph introduces the idea. The second paragraph adds enough ordinary sentences that a reader detector can tell this is an article and not a menu or a login form. Nothing here is private, and nothing here should be sent anywhere. The words stay in the browser unless a person explicitly saves a file or sends it to a server they configured themselves.

Listen Later should not appear on this page by itself. A test turns it on, checks that the player is there, and checks that a second visit to the same site can show the player only after the site was pinned. The save button should offer a download of a self-contained page, not a link that only makes sense to someone who already installed the extension.

Here is one more paragraph so the text is comfortably longer than a few captions. It talks about kettles, trains, and quiet rooms only as scenery. The point is the length and the paragraphs, not the subject. When the voice is on, each of these words can be highlighted in place without moving the pictures or the links that a real article would have.
