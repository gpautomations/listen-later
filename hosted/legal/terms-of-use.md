## What this service is

Listen Later can save a personal reading copy of a page you are already looking at. The hosted link is that copy, opened in a browser, with the words highlighted as the audio plays. It is a personal-use reading tool, like saving an article to read later in Pocket, Instapaper, or Speechify.

It is not a public library, a podcast directory, or a place to republish someone else's article.

## Who may use it

You may upload a page only if you have the right to access that page. That includes a page you can already read because it is public, because you are logged in, or because the rights holder allows personal listening. It does not include a page you reached by breaking access controls, and it does not include a page whose terms forbid this kind of copy when those terms apply to you.

You may open a link that was given to you. You may not scrape links, build a catalog, or frame the pages on another site.

## Your copy, and the original

The episode page shows the original article's address and a link back to it. The audio was generated on your computer. Listen Later does not claim rights in the underlying article. You keep responsibility for what you upload.

Do not upload something you know you have no right to copy. If you are unsure, do not upload it.

## Private links are not a login

The episode address is long and random. That is what keeps it off a public index. It is not a password prompt. Anyone who has the link can open the page. Do not post the link where you would not post the article itself.

The extension stores a secret on your browser that can list and delete your episodes. There is no email recovery in this version. If you remove the extension, you lose that secret. Delete episodes you do not want to leave online before you do that.

## What we may remove

We may delete an episode, or the whole account, if we believe it breaks these terms, if we receive a credible complaint, or if we have to. We do not promise to keep a copy. Deletion is how a report is handled.

## Takedown and DMCA

**A DMCA designated agent has not been registered with the U.S. Copyright Office.** Do not charge for this service until that registration is done and a lawyer has checked this section.

Until then, send a takedown request to the configured abuse address (placeholder: abuse@example.com). Include:

- the episode URL or the episode id
- the original work and where it lives
- your contact details
- a statement that you are the rights holder or their agent, and that you believe the use is not authorized
- your physical or electronic signature

The operator can delete an episode by id with the takedown tool in this repository. Deleting the episode removes the page, the audio, and the images from the bucket.

If you believe something was removed by mistake, write to the same address. Restoration is not promised.

A future registered agent should be named here, with the address that was filed, before these terms are published as binding.

## Acceptable use

You may not use the service to publish malware, to attack the service, to overload it, or to share login credentials. Quotas (size, count, and upload rate) are part of the service. Attempts to get around them can be blocked.

## No warranty

The extension is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later and comes with absolutely no warranty. The hosted service, once it is offered, should be offered the same way unless a lawyer writes a different warranty clause. Do not promise uptime, and do not promise that a link will keep working.
